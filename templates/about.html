About Playdex

What is Playdex?

Playdex is a personal game tracking and review app. It lets you keep track of the games you've played, rate them, and write your own reviews.

Why did I make it?

I made Playdex as a way to combine my interest in gaming with my interest in programming.

How does it work?

You can add games to your library, give them a rating out of 10, write a review, and edit or delete them later.

About the project

Playdex is built with Python, Flask, HTML, CSS and SQLite. It started as a local project and was later put online so it could be tested from different devices.

What's next?

Playdex is currently focused on being a simple personal game tracker. More features may be added in the future if they are actually useful.
